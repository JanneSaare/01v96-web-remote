# Code of conduct

This is a small, friendly project for people who like making mixing consoles easier to use. To keep it that way:

- Be kind and patient. People here have very different backgrounds: live sound, programming, or both.
- Assume good intent, and disagree about the ideas, not the person.
- No harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind.
- Keep reports and discussions on topic, and don't post other people's private information.

Maintainers may edit, close or remove contributions that don't follow this, and may block people who keep
breaking these rules. To report a problem, use GitHub's *Report content* feature or the private security
reporting described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
